SynthSight — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

SynthSight ("the App") is a private publishing tool operated by Pablo Vasta for SynthSight's own social media accounts (@synthsight_).

2. What data we access

The App only accesses the SynthSight TikTok account, authorised by its owner. It uses:

  • Basic account information (open ID, display name) to confirm the connected account.
  • Upload and publishing permissions to send SynthSight's own videos to that account.
  • Public metrics of SynthSight's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) to measure their performance.

The App does not access, collect or store data from other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

3. How data is stored

Access tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in a private GitHub repository and are only used by automated workflows to publish and read metrics. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the connection is active. Disconnecting the App in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions) revokes access immediately, and the stored tokens are then deleted.

SynthSight — Política de privacidad

Última actualización: 8 de noviembre de 2026

SynthSight es una herramienta privada de publicación gestionada por Pablo Vasta para las cuentas de SynthSight (@synthsight_). Solo accede a la cuenta de TikTok de SynthSight, autorizada por su titular: datos básicos de la cuenta, permisos para subir y publicar sus propios vídeos y las métricas públicas de esos vídeos. No accede a datos de otros usuarios, espectadores ni seguidores. Los tokens se guardan cifrados como secretos en un repositorio privado de GitHub, no se venden ni se comparten con terceros, y se eliminan al desconectar la app desde TikTok (Ajustes y privacidad → Seguridad y permisos → Permisos de apps y servicios). Contacto: info@vinnievasta.com
