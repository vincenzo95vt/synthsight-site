SynthSight ("the App") is a private publishing tool operated by Pablo Vasta for the SynthSight media accounts (@synthsight_). It uploads short explainer videos produced by SynthSight to SynthSight's own TikTok account, only after the account owner has reviewed and approved each video.

The App is not offered to the public. The only TikTok account connected to it is the SynthSight account, authorised by its owner through TikTok Login Kit.

All content published through the App is created by SynthSight. Third-party material, when used, is credited. The account owner is responsible for the content published and for complying with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

4. Revoking access

The account owner can disconnect the App at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
